TOUCHLINE Radar — product facts
Updated: 15 October 2026
Website: https://touchlineradar.com
Based in: Tbilisi, Georgia
Contact: contact@touchlineradar.com

TOUCHLINE Radar helps football players discover agents by registration country and agencies by office country. Registration and browsing are free. Players build private profiles containing their nationality, position, football links and PDFs. Signup supports Google or email/password; typed email registration does not require confirmation. The platform does not ask for age or date of birth.

Introductions cost USD 0.50 each and include saved profile PDFs. Messages are sent by TOUCHLINE Radar with replies directed to the player's registered email. One successful introduction per player and recorded agency destination is allowed.

Contact credits reveal a published email and any available phone number. Contact bundles: 5/USD 2.50, 20/USD 9, 50/USD 20, 100/USD 35. Contact credits and introduction credits have separate balances. There is no subscription.

PayPal and a PayPal-hosted card checkout option are available; guest card availability depends on PayPal eligibility. Apple Pay and Google Pay are not enabled.

The directory uses published source snapshots and technically checked emails. Active inboxes and current licences are not guaranteed. An introduction does not guarantee a reply, representation or a contract. Do not describe directory listings as partnerships or endorsements.
